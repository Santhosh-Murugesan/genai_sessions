10 POPULAR MOVIES OF PRITHVIRAJ SUKUMARAN

1. LUCIFER (2019)
Released: 28 March 2019. Director: Prithviraj Sukumaran; Writer: Murali Gopy; Producer: Antony Perumbavoor. Cast: Mohanlal, Prithviraj Sukumaran, Manju Warrier, Tovino Thomas, Vivek Oberoi and Indrajith Sukumaran. Reported budget: about ₹30 crore. Reported worldwide gross: about ₹129 crore. The film had a long and highly successful theatrical run, although an exact all-theatre day count is not consistently documented. Message: political power can corrupt institutions, and people must be alert to manipulation and injustice. Lucifer was also Prithviraj's directorial debut.

2. ENNU NINTE MOIDEEN (2015)
Released: 19 September 2015. Director: R. S. Vimal; Writer: R. S. Vimal; Producer: Suresh Raj, Binoy Shankarath and others. Cast: Prithviraj Sukumaran, Parvathy Thiruvothu, Tovino Thomas, Bala, Lena and Sai Kumar. Estimated budget: about ₹13 crore. Reported worldwide gross: around ₹50 crore. The movie had an exceptional theatrical run of more than 150 days. Message: true love, sacrifice and commitment can survive social barriers, but society and circumstances can have devastating consequences.

3. SALAAR: PART 1 – CEASEFIRE (2023)
Released: 22 December 2023. Director and Writer: Prashanth Neel; Producer: Vijay Kiragandur. Cast: Prabhas, Prithviraj Sukumaran, Shruti Haasan, Jagapathi Babu, Bobby Simha and Sriya Reddy. Reported budget: around ₹270 crore, although figures vary by source. Reported worldwide gross estimates vary widely, roughly ₹600–700 crore. It had a strong multi-week theatrical run, with an exact day count varying by territory and source. Message: friendship, loyalty and promises can become stronger than political power and violence.

4. AADUJEEVITHAM: THE GOAT LIFE (2024)
Released: 28 March 2024. Director: Blessy; Writer: Blessy; Producers: Blessy, Steven Adams and others. Cast: Prithviraj Sukumaran, Jimmy Jean-Louis, Amala Paul, KR Gokul and Talib al Balushi. Reported budget: about ₹82 crore. Reported worldwide gross: approximately ₹157.6 crore. It enjoyed a sustained theatrical run of several weeks, though a single reliable all-territory day count is not publicly standardized. Message: human resilience, hope and the will to survive can overcome extreme exploitation and hardship. The film is based on Benyamin's novel about Najeeb.

5. AYYAPPANUM KOSHIYUM (2020)
Released: 7 February 2020. Director and Writer: Sachy; Producers: Ranjith and P. M. Sasidharan. Cast: Prithviraj Sukumaran, Biju Menon, Gowri Nandha, Anna Reshma Rajan and Ranjith. Reported budget estimates are around ₹5 crore, while some industry sources give different figures. Reported worldwide gross is around ₹36–44 crore depending on the source. The film had a successful theatrical run before the COVID-19 disruption affected cinema operations. Message: uncontrolled ego, pride and social status can turn small conflicts into destructive battles.

6. DRIVING LICENCE (2019)
Released: 20 December 2019 in India. Director: Lal Jr.; Writer: Sachy; Producers: Supriya Menon and Listin Stephen. Cast: Prithviraj Sukumaran, Suraj Venjaramoodu, Miya George, Deepti Sati, Saiju Kurup and Nandhu. Reported budget: around ₹10 crore. The film crossed ₹30 crore in domestic box-office collections and was reported to be running successfully for more than two months. Message: fame, ego and personal pride can damage relationships, while humility and mutual respect can resolve conflicts.

7. KADUVA (2022)
Released: 7 July 2022. Director: Shaji Kailas; Writer: Jinu V. Abraham; Producers: Supriya Menon and Listin Stephen. Cast: Prithviraj Sukumaran, Vivek Oberoi, Samyuktha Menon, Arjun Ashokan, Alencier Ley Lopez and Baiju Santhosh. Reported budget: around ₹17 crore. Worldwide gross: over ₹50 crore. It had a multi-week theatrical run and became one of the year's notable Malayalam box-office performers. Message: arrogance, power and revenge can create destructive cycles, and uncontrolled ego can affect families and communities.

8. JANA GANA MANA (2022)
Released: 28 April 2022. Director: Dijo Jose Antony; Writer: Sharis Mohammed; Producers: Supriya Menon and Listin Stephen. Cast: Prithviraj Sukumaran, Suraj Venjaramoodu, Mamta Mohandas, Sri Divya, G. M. Sundar and Dhruvan. Estimated budget: about ₹10 crore. Reported worldwide collection figures vary, with industry estimates placing it around the ₹50 crore range. It enjoyed a strong theatrical run for a Malayalam social-justice drama. Message: justice, media influence, political accountability and public opinion must be handled responsibly in a democracy.

9. MUMBAI POLICE (2013)
Released: 3 May 2013. Director: Rosshan Andrrews; Writers: Bobby and Sanjay; Producer: Nisad Haneefa. Cast: Prithviraj Sukumaran, Jayasurya, Rahman, Aparna Nair and Riyaz Khan. Reported budget: about ₹5.75 crore. Reported worldwide gross: approximately ₹17 crore. The movie became a notable box-office success, although a precise theatrical day count is not consistently recorded. Message: truth can be uncomfortable, identity and memory can be complicated, and justice requires confronting even painful personal realities.

10. CLASSMATES (2006)
Released: 25 August 2006. Director: Lal Jose; Writer: James Albert; Producers: Prakash Damodaran and P. K. Muralidharan. Cast: Prithviraj Sukumaran, Narain, Kavya Madhavan, Indrajith Sukumaran, Jayasurya and Radhika. Reported budget: about ₹2.02 crore. The film became a major box-office success and completed a 150-day theatrical run, with 100-day runs reported in several important centres. Message: friendship, memories, love and unresolved emotions can remain powerful long after college life ends.

NOTE: Box-office budgets and worldwide collections for older Indian films often differ between trade trackers, producer statements and media reports. The figures above are therefore presented as commonly reported/estimated figures. Exact theatrical running days are not publicly standardized for every film, so only well-documented runs are stated precisely.
